My Azida,

Today isn't really about a date on the calendar. It's just an excuse for me to stop everything and say, out loud, how lucky I feel that out of everyone, I get you.

You have this way of making the small moments feel important, and the hard days feel lighter, just because you're in them with me. The way you laugh with your whole face. The way you show up for the people you love without being asked. The way you can turn an ordinary Tuesday into something I'll remember. I notice all of it, even when I don't say it out loud.

I love the version of me that exists when I'm with you — softer, calmer, a little more brave. You didn't just find a place in my life, you became the reason so much of it makes sense.

So here's my little corner of the internet, built stone by stone with you in mind. It's not a fancy gift. It's just the truth, dressed up a little: I adore you, I'm proud of you, and I'm endlessly grateful you're mine.

Here's to more ordinary days that somehow never feel ordinary with you in them.

Yours, today and every day after
